## Current acceptance status

The first sealed full Chromium export contained 563 cells and established the Session 11 worklist. Targeted
product and source-truth reruns have since produced substantial correctness and algorithmic progress, recorded
in the notes below, but they are not a substitute for a new complete rotation. Three invocation/platform
boundaries are documented in the boundary audit; they are not Session 11 DoD exemptions and still require an
honest harness-side resolution and fresh result.

Session 11 remains **in progress**. Do not claim completion until all of the following are recorded together:

- a fresh full Chromium rotation with 0 FAIL, ERROR, timeout, or OOM and no unapproved NAs;
- rotation-wide `n≥5` warm wall and peak-memory parity, or a current cell-specific ADR-backed exemption;
- the #1 conformance-then-coverage aggregate and per-family scorecard;
- no PASS-to-FAIL in Chromium and WebKit, plus the inherited Firefox smoke/correctness gate;
- `bun run gate`, coverage at least 90%, anti-cheat, force-software determinism, and package budgets green.
